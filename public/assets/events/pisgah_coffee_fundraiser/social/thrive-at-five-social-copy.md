This Friday: raise a cup for community connection.

Join SparkPoint and Pisgah Coffee Roasters for Thrive @ Five, the release party for Common Ground Resilience Roast.

Friday, July 31

Pisgah Coffee Roasters
6283 Asheville Hwy, Pisgah Forest

Live music from Mark & Sally Wingate, food, drinks, friends, and the first pour of a roast created to help fuel SparkPoint's new Resilience Hub in downtown Brevard.
