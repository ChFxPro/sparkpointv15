How to use your trip promo kit

Thank you for sharing the Gala’s eight trips with the people you know. Everything here is ready to go: pick the piece that suits the conversation.

What’s inside

  • A leave-behind, the next sheet in this file. The front shows all eight trips with photos and prices; the back answers the questions guests ask most.
  • Nine digital cards, in the shared Drive folder SP Gala 26 › 09 Board trip promo: one image of all eight trips, and one card for each trip.
  • The leave-behind in three versions, in the same folder, listed at the foot of this page.

How to use it

  • Text one trip. Send the card for the trip that fits the person, like the place they’ve always wanted to see.
  • Show all eight. Send the overview image, or email this file.
  • Hand it over. Print the leave-behind double-sided and give it, or leave it behind. Keep this cover for yourself.
  • Point them to the films. The QR code and link open the Gala page, with a short film of every trip and tickets.

The short version

  • Every trip is available to every guest at a set price, and more than one guest can choose the same trip.
  • Prices are for the whole package, for two (Cortona for four). Airfare and local taxes and fees aren’t included.
  • Travel any time within three years, with no blackout dates.
  • Part of every trip’s price is a tax-deductible gift to SparkPoint, and a portion helps bring the Transylvania Resilience Hub to life.
  • Guests choose their trip at the Gala on Friday, December 11. Tickets are $100 per person.

Good to keep in mind

  • Keep the photo credits. Each card names its photographer, as the photo’s licence requires, so please don’t crop it off.
  • Talk about choosing at the Gala on December 11: that’s the night to choose.
  • Not sure of an answer? Note the question and pass it to the SparkPoint team rather than guessing.

Good to know

◇

Choose your trip at the Gala. Every trip is available to every guest at a set price, and more than one guest can choose the same trip.

◇

Paying: in full, at the Gala or by the following week at the latest. Payment is by credit card, and you can split it across more than one card.

◇

Prices don’t include airfare or local taxes and fees, such as hotel, tourist or port taxes, which vary by destination. Each trip covers hotels, tours and transfers on the ground (for Alaska, the cruise and a night in Seattle).

◇

Travel any time within three years, with no blackout dates. You choose your dates with a travel specialist.

◇

Who can go: the Cortona apartment is for four, in any mix of adults and children. On the trips for two, children 18 and under, or adults with disabilities, can be added for an extra charge when you book.

◇

Your own vacation, never a big tour group: most tours are small-group and some are private.

◇

Travel insurance isn’t included. Optional travel insurance is offered when you book, or you can use your own.

◇

It can be a gift. The person who chooses the trip at the Gala can transfer it to someone else.
