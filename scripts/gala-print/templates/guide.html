The brand in short

  • The name: A World of Connections (plural). In a sentence: "Join us for A World of Connections, the SparkPoint Gala." Casually, "the Gala".
  • Tagline: "Every journey connects back home."
  • Lockup byline: THE SPARKPOINT GALA over TRAVEL EXPERIENCE.
  • Typeface: Josefin Sans only (free, Google Fonts). Use capitals with wide letter-spacing for labels. Set the tagline light, with connects back home. in semibold brass. No Fraunces.
  • Look: an Art Deco travel poster. Frames are a double brass hairline. Buttons are square, SparkPoint pink with a thin brass border and white capitals.
  • Trips are numbered as connections: "Connection 01 · Greece", "Eight connections, one evening."
  • Retired: "An Atlas of Wonders", the "Atlas Gala" nickname, atlas plates, Fraunces, "See the world. Strengthen home.", "live travel auction".
  • Keep off every piece: the travel provider's name, internal trip prices or SparkPoint's share, and any unconfirmed giveback percentage.
